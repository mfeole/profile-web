About

I'm a data scientist with 8+ years of experience turning data into models and knowledge for better decision-making. I combine mathematical, statistical, and computational modeling with hands-on engineering, and I care about shipping tools that work in production.

My recent work centers on generative AI: LLMs, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), agents, and chatbots that automate internal processes, uncover inefficiencies, and put software to the test. My path is diverse by design: at every crossroads I chose the project that interested me most, and the common thread has always been data, machine learning, and AI.

Beyond work, I meditate, play guitar, read widely, and write essays in my newsletter, Navegarte.
